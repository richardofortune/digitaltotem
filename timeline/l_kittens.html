Building a home!

In 2012, my wife-to-be and I started talking about getting a pet. Living in a rental, we realised that finding a place we liked that also allowed pets would be difficult. The conversation soon turned to buying our own place as we felt it was the sensible next step in our relationship. So, after 6 months of looking, we found a house we liked; a tired 85 year old home that only ever had two owners. Two sets of families had raised a family here and now it was our turn. We moved in on a Friday and by Sunday afternoon we had adopted not one, but two cats (Myrtle & Badger).

Thus we refer to our furry little friends as the most expensive cats in the world.
