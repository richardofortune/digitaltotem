How-To

Filtering the themes

The story panels are divided into themes. You can filter them using the buttons on the main page.

  1. [●] Life Events
  2. [●] Work Achievements
  3. [●] Pivotal life experiences
  4. [●] Passion projects

Navigation

Navigating via the Story Panels

You can use the left and right keys on your keyboard to quickly navigate through the Story Panels.

Navigating using the Timeline

Click on a node(red dot) in the timeline to skip to that Story Panel.

Navigating on a mobile device

You can use your mobile device to view the content. This experience was built for desktop (for shame Richard!!) and as a result features such as the timeline with not be active. But hey, we all to know perfect is the enemy of done, so maybe next time! :)
