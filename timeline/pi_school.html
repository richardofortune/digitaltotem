Pioneering educators!

In 1981, when it came to sending me to school my parents wanted me be to be in the ~4% of Irish people who could speak our native tongue fluently. As luck would have it a pioneering young woman named Brìd DeRoiste had decided to set up a Gaelic school in my home town. Taking a chance my parents enrolled me in this new venture. This decision set me on an educational path very different from many others in my town. Because of our success at the school, a subsequent pioneering educator was given the chance to setup a Gaelic highschool in our town. This translated into many great experiences such as meeting the president of Ireland (twice!) and the opportunity to leave school speaking 4 languages. I won't go so far as to say I loved school, I actually found aspects of it very difficult, but I on reflection I definitely love the experience I had.
