Pioneering educators!

In 1981, when it came to sending me to school my parents wanted me be to be in the ~4% of Irish people who could speak our native tongue fluently. As luck would have it a pioneering young woman named Brìd DeRoiste had decided to set up a Gaelic school in my home town, and so taking a chance my parents enrolled me in this new venture. (If you’re curious, I’m the blond goofball on the far left)
