Not all life experiences are good

I really did wonder whether or not to put this story in here. But ultimately I felt I had to, for many reasons, but primarily because I cannot speak about significant life events and not mention the passing of my dear dad. In Feb this year my family lost a really wonderful father/husband/friend. Thankfully I had enough warning to get home and to be with him and support my family before he passed and the experience went as well as something like this could.

But the other reason I wanted to share this event with you, is that I think you would have liked him. When I was tidying up some of his affairs, I found the little Dale Carnegie book you see pictured above. When I asked dad about this in the hospital he told me that he had been sent to a Dale Carnegie event back in 1964 and that he received the booklet then. Obviously the content resonated, as dad kept it safely filed away and continued to collect self help books and motivational quotes throughout his life.

In the Pop-up image for this tile is a quote I found in one of dads last notebooks.

"If you could achieve one goal in your life within 24 hours, which goal would have the greatest impact on your life?"

My application to work at Buffer is my response to that question.
