Fish out of water

In 1998 I spent a year studying in Brittany, France for my Diplôme universitaire de technologie. As part of that qualification I needed to undertake work experience with a French company. Through some luck and a bit of hustle, I was fortunate enough to land a placement working for the French military in one of their research academies. This meant that I got to live and work on a military base for 4 months. (An experience I will never forget!)

My key projects whilst there were to build a secure intranet for the school so that students could work remotely, and to investigate 3d modelling tools for use in their modelling classes. Pretty much my dream job, working on the web and learning about 3d modelling!

Fun fact: On the weekends I'd get to leave the base, on more than one occasion when returning to base on a Monday morning our naval shuttle was caught in a "traffic jam" behind the Aircraft Carrier or a submarine. This was a time before selfies, although I did try to get a photo with a film camera. On reflection, probably not the smartest thing for me to be doing :)
