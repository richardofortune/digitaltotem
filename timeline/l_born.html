On this day!

My late arrival as last child meant that I missed all of the sibling rivalry in our house. While my siblings grew up with the usual spats, I grew up in wide-eyed awe of them. From one brother I developed a love of comedy, from my sister I developed an appreciation for being sensitive to the needs of others, from another brother I learned respect for creativity and finally from my eldest brother I discovered an aptitude for technology. A rather lucky way to start life, to be honest.

Being youngest also had the added benefit of a lack of desire to flaunt ego, which fortunately left space for me to focus on learning/emulating the best of each of them. This situation also helped me to develop a taste for adventure; with so many well trodden paths before me I was driven to make my own way.
