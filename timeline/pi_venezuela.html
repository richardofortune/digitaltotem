Hiking in Venezuela

In 2001 on a whim I decided to visit Venezuela with 2 friends.

It was the trip of a lifetime with so many many great experiences. But one that stands out to me was an experience I had whilst on a 3 day hike in the Canaima National Park.

It was the longest hike I'd done and given our lack of experience we thought it prudent to hire a guide. Our guide took care of most of the details for the trip, we simply had to carry our own packs and our share of the equipment.
