Getting Married in a fern house!

In 2014, I married my beautiful wife Molly in a fern house in rural NZ. We had close family and friends there to share in the moment and we then spent the rest of the day on a friends' sheep shearing farm doing fun stuff like easter egg hunts, dog trials, a table quiz and just a little bit of schmalzy speech giving.

It was a wonderful celebration and a memory that I cherish dearly. We're now plotting our next adventure together..stay tuned!
