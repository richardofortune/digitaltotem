The practice of giving thanks

In 2011, I began to notice that I was taking some of the better things in my life for granted and that this, in turn, was leaving me feeling unsatisfied. As an antidote, to this I decided to undertake a project of conscious giving of thanks. The Maori word for gratitude is Whakamihi and it seemed fitting to call my practice of thanks giving by a local name.

Every day for 30 days I documented my daily gratitude on Tumblr (haha!). I also took a photo each day to represent the thing for which I was giving gratitude. This project not only gave me reason to move past my previous state of mind, but also stretched me creatively.
