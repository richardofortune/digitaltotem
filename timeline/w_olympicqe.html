Olympic Class Quality Engineers @ Xero

In 2013 at Xero, the gauntlet was thrown and the development team was tasked with preparing our product so it could scale from 150,000 to 1 million customers and beyond. In parallel to this we also needed to roll out continuous delivery across the organisation in order to keep in step with the feature demands of new and existing customers.

I had observed, not just at Xero, but in other organisations where I'd worked, that QA quite often felt their reason for being was to find problems as opposed to creating value.

People in the industry seemed tired; many of them were doing the same work they'd always done. I felt we could inspire QA at Xero to look at their role differently, but not only that, we could also use this perspective to attract the kind of talent we wanted, too. Thus, the notion of Olympic Class Quality Engineers(QE) was born.

The premise was simple enough; treat our discipline like we would a competitive team sport, where we focused on training together and helping each other get to the next level.

We worked as a team to identify and resolve problems in our domain, treating Quality Engineering itself as the product we were delivering. We aimed to produce results as early as we could to the rest of the organisation so that we could ensure we were on the right track. Initiatives and upskilling in new areas such as security testing/usability testing and more kicked off and we quickly began to see a vibrant energy return to the team again.

The enthusiasm we now see in QE at Xero is unlike any environment I've been in. People are really loving what they do and we're attracting people who are drawn to that.
