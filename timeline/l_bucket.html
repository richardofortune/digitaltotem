Bucket list by 30

I spent most of my 20's working long hours and enjoying life with my friends.

But over time this life began to lose its lustre, I appreciated all that I had, but also felt this deep sense of dissatisfaction that I couldn't ignore.

Although I didn't know what direction was best, rather than shy away from the problem I decided to map out my bucket list and see how much of it I could action in one trip.

There were many items on the list, but a few bubbled to the top and formed a cohesive journey. These were:

  • Hear Mongolian throat singing in person
  • Travel on the Trans Siberian Railway
  • Live in New Zealand for a year
  • Leave IT and try something compeltely different. (I ended up making a line of hoodies!)

Leaving my friends and my career in IT left me with gaps in my identity, it was disconcerting at time, however as my journey progressed I faced these gaps, had many adventures and learned so much more about who I am.

Some people consider sights to be the key bucket list items, for me, I've learned it will always be through experiences that we yield the greatest value.
