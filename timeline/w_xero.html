Xero the Startup

In 2010, I joined Xero as the Quality Engineer on their public facing API. I was employee number 70 in what is now a 1350 person global business. From the outset, I focused on automation as the pathway to success for our team. We were the first team at Xero to have end to end automated testing. Due to the fact we had a more mature development process we were afforded the time to dig in and work on organisation wide initiatives that helped us grow as a company. I led initiatives to select test tools and intranet software. I championed treating our automation as a first class citizen, rather than a hack we put in place to buy time. We moved from QA led quality to team led quality and I worked on other team growth initiatives such as Olympic Class Quality Engineers and Next Levels, which you can read about elswhere on this site.

Being part of the Xero journey has been the experience of a lifetime. The technical challenges we've had to tackle have been invigorating and the culture problems we've dealt with due to nearly 100% year-on-year team expansion, have taught me much about what I love about coming to work. Simply put, technical problems can be found almost everywhere, but working with people to solve technical and human problems together is what fires my passion. I've learned to think about the team as the product we're building and the software being merely a secondary , albeit valuable output.
