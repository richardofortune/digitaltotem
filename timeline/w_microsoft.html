2001 - Microsoft

In 2000 I had my dream job, I worked for Havas interactive, testing video games such as Echo the Dolphin and Counterstrike. It was as much fun as you might expect, but I felt it lacked a certain direction. In modern times working for MS is like working for Yawn Enterprises Inc., however back then MSN was doing really great things on the web and in Ireland the team there were taking the MSN product and making it relevant in 32 markets across the globe. It was an opportunity to move from swimming in a comfortable stream to cutting my teeth in a roaring torrent.
