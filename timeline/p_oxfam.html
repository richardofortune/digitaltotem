The Oxfam trailwalk

In 2011 my wife and I decided to take part in the Oxfam 100km long distance walk, a "race" in which you and your team must complete 100km(62miles) in under 36hours. On the surface it may not appear to be the most physically challenging event. However once we assembled our team, and began our practice walks we not only learned about the complexities of walking at a common pace but also the art of positive thinking. The walk itself is a round the clock race so you end up staying up all night when taking part. At 3am in the morning morale can take a bit of a hit.

But we had a really wonderful team, and in the weeks leading up to the event our training walks not only helped us prepare physically but also helped us bond as friends. We ended up talking about a world of topics on those training walks! Our team, the Charming Marchers raised $7736 for charity, something that made it all the more worthwhile.

But ultimately the experience itself was the greatest reward, when we crossed the line after 24 hours of walking I told myself "Never, ever again!", but now I think I might just be ready to give it another go! :)
