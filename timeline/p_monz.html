Makers Org NZ

In 2010 I decided to commit to my love of product design and learn how to create physical objects. I noticed pretty quickly that I lacked the skills necessary to take some of my projects to completion so I started a Meetup group to connect with others who might be on a similar path. At the Meetup we met and we spoke about the projects we were working on.

It was fun and social, but pretty soon I noted that there were common themes to problems we all faced. Usually around skills, access to tools or having a work space. I also noted that people often came to the meetups out of curiosity and a desire to make, but never really progressed much further passed being observers.

There was a clear problem here for a lot of people and it was at this point that I came up with a theory which I labelled "People, Projects & Place" - I had learned from my meetups that bringing people together in a place only gave them passive enjoyment and in order to bring the community together they needed a common goal or a project to focus on. I tested this theory with some small projects, I setup a tiny makerspace where people could have a sense of place, I then crowdfunded the purchase of a community 3dprinter kit so that we had a common project to work on and I continued to run the meetups so that more and more people came to share and learn. We used our community 3dprinter to run free public workshops on 3d printing, and in our first year we trained 90 people who had never 3d modelled or printed how to use the machine, all for free.

From this experience I noted that this problem wasn't unique to my region, but that we needed more of these spaces nationally. This wasn't a unique phenomenon, it was happening in parallel with the global maker movement, but in the Northern Hemishpere access to space and tools was a lot cheaper and thus we needed a solution that would work in a New Zealand context. It is here that the origins of Makers Org New Zealand(MoNZ) began. Seeing that our best chance of levelling up in this space would be through collaboration and shared effort MoNZ set about building the tools for open and shared innovation infrastructure in New Zealand.

It has been an amazing journey over the last 5+ years, MoNZ has grown and is making an impact - we have a distributed community of volunteers who are working on some amazing initiatives. Sadly though I have yet to become the product designer I dreamed I would! :)
