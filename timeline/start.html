How-To

Filtering the themes

The story panels are divided into themes. You can filter them using the buttons on the main page.

  1. [●] Life Events
  2. [●] Work Achievements
  3. [●] Pivotal Life Experiences
  4. [●] Passion Projects

Navigation

Navigating via the Story Panels

You can use the left() and right() keys on your keyboard to quickly navigate through the Story Panels.

Navigating using the Timeline

Click on a node(red dot) in the timeline to skip to that Story Panel. Use the left and right "arrows" to advance the timeline. (This doesn't work as well as I'd like, and for this I have much shame but I did want to maintain a sense of timing through the stories, so it stayed in, in it's less than perfect form.)

Navigating on a mobile device

You can use your mobile device to browse and view the content, however this experience was built to be enjoyed on desktop and as a result the mobile version may be less than optimal. (In a perfect world this would work across all platforms. However perfect is the enemy of done, so maybe next time! :)
