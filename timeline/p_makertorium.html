If you build it, they will come, by the thousands!

In 2011 I had a vision to one day run a public maker event that could be used to introduce people to this wonderful new movement I felt part of. I'd been to Makerfaire in New York and dreamed of something similar happening here. In 2013 myself and a team of others held the first ever national maker event in New Zealand. Not only did we manage to secure the national museum as a venue, but we ended up hosting around 10000 people on the day.

It was an amazing event, and bless the good people at the museum, but I'm not sure they knew what they were letting themselves in for when they let us use their space. We had robot sitars, rub goldberg machines, sumo wrestling robots, interactive native bird installations, the list goes on and on!

We brought people from all across New Zealand together for the first time, we built connections that previously didn't exist and we also managed to help a local team for wikihouse secure fundng to build their first fullscale prototype, which was then shipped back down south inside the MakerCrate! (how meta!)

Makertorium was a delight, and although a huge investment of time, how often in your life do you get handed the master key to a national museum for a night?! (Along with Ben Stiller'esque hilarity!)
