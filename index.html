Richard Fortune

Buffer wanna-be & All-round positive soul

Hi there team Buffer! I'm reaching out to you because I think the work you do is pretty special. It was your product that drew me in initially, but it was your culture that kept me reading your blog posts week after week! I'm going to cut straight to the chase; I would like the opportunity to contribute to the journey you are on. In fact, I would like nothing more than to invest my heart and energy in to exploring what success looks like not only for Buffer users, but for the Buffer team, too. However, rather than take the usual job application approach and make a bunch of assumptions, or try to pitch you a list of my achievements, I've decided to present you with some of the key points in my life so that you can see if I'm the kind of person you might like to work with. If it turns out that you think I am we should definitely talk some more!

Here is my life in the form of story tiles plotted along a timeline, which you can consume in chronological order or in small filtered collections depending on what themes you are drawn to. My goal was to give a taste of who I am and where I've been and rather than write a bogstandard cover letter, I thought I'd put my life in a format that is a little bit more fun. Like pizza, this is a dish best shared, and like pizza, it's definitely not expected that you finish it all in one sitting. However, if you do, I will think you're pretty awesome!
